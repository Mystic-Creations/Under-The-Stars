## Description
Under The Stars is a camping mod mainly focused on exploring, food and spending time with your friends while gazing upon the stars above
while enjoying the scenery of the Minecraft world at night.<br>
Sitting around the campfire, telling stories or just skipping the night in your sleeping bag; you choose what you do at your camp.
